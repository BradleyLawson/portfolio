# **Troubleshooting**

Troubleshooting is a critical part of maintaining stable and secure cloud infrastructures. I have gained critical experience in diagnosing and resolving issues across various aspects of cloud environments, from networking and security to performance optimization and automation failures. I utilized every tool at my disposal to diagnose and resolve issues that arose. These tools would include internal documentation, collaboration, and research to find solutions to common problems.

## **Key Troubleshooting Areas**

I have addressed a wide range of challenges in cloud environments, working across different systems and services to ensure security, reliability and uptime. These are some of the key areas where I have focused my troubleshooting efforts:

### 1. **Network Failures**
- **VNet and Subnet Misconfigurations**: Diagnosed and resolved issues related to Virtual Networks (VNets) and subnets, ensuring proper communication between services and applications.
- **Network Security Group Conflicts**: Troubleshot and resolved conflicts in Network Security Groups (NSGs) and firewall rules that were blocking legitimate traffic or causing unnecessary latency.
- **DNS Resolution Failures**: Resolved through Deploy if Not Exists Policy Private DNS resolution for Azure Cognitive Services.

### 2. **Security and Compliance**
- **Version Maintenance**: Researched and documented up to date versions of applications for Azure Resuources. Utilized tools such as Azure Resource Graph Explorer with KQL to gain insight of our current enviroment. Upgraded / collaborated with other departements in assisting upgrading to the current stable versions.
- **Compliance Violations**: Identified non-compliant resources by creating and monitoring Azure Policies, remediating issues such as public IP exposure and insecure storage, and bringing resources back into compliance.

### 3. **Automation Failures**
- **Failed CI/CD Pipeline Deployments**: Debugged failures in Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines by identifying errors in deployment scripts, misconfigured environments, or incorrect versions of dependencies.
- **Terraform and IaC Issues**: Resolved Infrastructure as Code (IaC) issues related to Terraform, such as state file conflicts, resource drift, and failed deployments due to version mismatches or incorrect configurations.

### 4. **Operational and FinOps**
- **Decomissioned Unneeded Subscriptions and Resources**: Researched unused, unneeded, and underutilzed resources and subscriptions and decommissioned them.

## **Approach to Troubleshooting**

Troubleshooting cloud environments requires a structured and methodical approach. I rely on a combination of proactive monitoring, analysis, and collaboration with cross-functional teams to identify root causes and implement effective solutions.

### 1. **Root Cause Analysis**
- **In-depth Analysis**: Analyzed systems, applications, and logs to find the origin of issues and root cause.

### 2. **Collaboration for Resolution**
- **Cross-Departmental Collaboration**: Worked closely with all stakeholders to troubleshoot issues, leveraging expertise from different areas to resolve complex challenges.
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Kept stakeholders informed of the troubleshooting process, providing timely updates and recommendations for future prevention.

## **Notable Troubleshooting Successes**

### 1. **Remediated Compliance and Security Vulnerabilities**
- Created policy to mitigate key security misconfigurations that exposed critical resources to public networks. By identifying the misconfigured Networks Settings, I quickly applied fixes and implemented monitoring policies to prevent similar incidents.

### 2. **Optimized Cloud Spend by Identifying Underutilized Resources**
- Investigated high cloud costs and identified underutilized resources that were contributing to unnecessary spend. Recommended scaling policies and decommissioned unused resources, reducing costs by a significant margin.

## **Conclusion**

My experience in troubleshooting cloud environments has been essential to ensuring stability, performance, and security. Through a combination of proactive monitoring, methodical analysis, and collaboration, I have successfully resolved a wide range of issues, helping businesses maintain seamless cloud operations.
